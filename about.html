Our Story

Real People. Real Hauls. Real Commitments.

ABBA Global Corp was founded with a simple idea: oilfield freight should be as dependable as the operations it supports. Based in Kermit, Texas, we serve frac sand and oilfield logistics across West Texas and Southeast New Mexico.

We're a diversity-owned carrier — and a Permian operator first. Our drivers know these roads. Our team picks up the phone. From ABBA to Z, we deliver with ease.
