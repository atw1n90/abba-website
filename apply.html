How You Get Paid

Straight Pay. No Surprises.

What you earn, what we cover, and what gets deducted — laid out plainly.

Driver
30% of weekly delivered gross
  • ABBA covers: fuel, IFTA, plates
  • You need: SandTrax, H2S, and PEC certifications (waived if active)
  • Local and regional only
  • Sleeper-cab tractors
Owner-Operator
92% of weekly delivered gross — above-market split
  • Standard deductions: fuel, IFTA, plates, SandTrax / H2S / PEC certs (waived if active)
  • Passed through 100%: fuel savings and fuel surcharges to your weekly settlement
  • Local and regional only
Performance Bonuses

When ABBA earns performance bonuses from our partners, we pass them through to owner-operators — 100%.

Contracting Requirements

What You'll Need to Contract with ABBA.

Drivers & Owner-Operators

  • Class A CDL with current medical card
  • 23 years or older
  • 1–2 years driving experience minimum
  • Clean MVR (last 3 years)
  • Authorized to work in the U.S.

Owner-Operators Also Need

  • Clean truck inspections

No endorsements required — we contract for frac sand (wet & dry) only.
