COMPANY OVERVIEW

ABBA Global Corp (AGC) is a bilingual, Hispanic-owned power-only carrier headquartered in Kermit, TX. We were founded on a simple idea: the freight that keeps a well producing should be as dependable as the well itself. Nine ABBA tractors plus partner capacity move 100+ loads of frac sand weekly across the Delaware and Midland sub-basins — with an owner behind the wheel and a partner who runs the business.
