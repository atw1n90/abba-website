ABBA Global Corp

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

1. Who we are

ABBA Global Corp ("ABBA," "we," "us") is a motor carrier based in Texas. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through abbaglobalcorp.com (the "Site"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: info@abbaglobalcorp.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our Driver / Owner Operator Interest Form or otherwise contact us, we collect what you enter, which may include: name, phone number, email address, city and state, preferred language, whether you are applying as a driver or owner operator, date available, CDL status, years of experience, CDL state and expiration, whether you are 23 or older, work authorization status, whether you have had license suspensions or major violations in the last three years, oilfield experience, tractor and trailer information (owner operators), whether you are leased to another carrier, and any comments you provide.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the Site may collect device and usage information such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, and the approximate location derived from your IP address, using cookies and similar technologies (see Section 6).

Information from others. If you move forward in our process, we may receive information about you from third parties as described in Section 4.

3. How we use information

  • To review your interest form and contact you about driver or owner operator opportunities.
  • To send you a full application and onboarding documents (electronically, through our e-signature provider) if you are selected to continue.
  • To evaluate qualifications and, with your separate written authorization, to obtain background, driving-record, employment-verification, and FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse information as required by U.S. Department of Transportation regulations (49 CFR Parts 382 and 391).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site, and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, insurance, and safety obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, such as Google (form storage in Google Sheets/Drive), our e-signature provider, email/SMS providers, and website hosting.
  • Consumer reporting agencies and screening vendors, only after you sign a separate authorization, to obtain background checks, motor vehicle records, and prior-employer safety performance history. Reports about you are governed by the Fair Credit Reporting Act; you will receive the required disclosures and a summary of your rights before any report is obtained.
  • Insurers, brokers, shippers, and regulators where required for qualification, safety, audit, or legal compliance (for example, the FMCSA or your state licensing agency).
  • Legal and safety disclosures when required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers if ABBA is involved in a merger, sale, or reorganization.

5. Text messages, calls, and email

By checking the consent box on our interest form you agree that ABBA may contact you by phone, text message, or email about opportunities. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out of texts, or email us to stop calls or emails. Consent is not a condition of working with ABBA.

6. Cookies and advertising

We advertise on Google and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) to reach drivers. Those platforms may place cookies or pixels on the Site to measure whether an ad led to a visit or form submission and to show you relevant ads. You can control this through your browser settings, Google's ad settings (adssettings.google.com), Meta's ad preferences, and industry opt-out tools such as optout.aboutads.info. We do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals because there is no common standard for them.

7. How long we keep information

We keep interest-form and application information for up to three years, consistent with DOT driver-qualification record practices, and then delete or de-identify it unless a longer period is required by law, insurance, or an ongoing legal matter. If you become an ABBA driver or owner operator, your records are kept for the periods required by 49 CFR Part 391 and other applicable rules.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including access controls and reputable cloud providers, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure; please contact us right away if you believe your information has been compromised.

9. Your choices and rights

You may email us to access, correct, or delete the information you have provided, or to withdraw your consent to be contacted. We will honor requests as required by law and may need to verify your identity. Depending on where you live (for example, Texas or California), you may have additional rights under state privacy law, including the right to know what we collect and to opt out of targeted advertising; you can exercise these by emailing info@abbaglobalcorp.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Children

The Site and our opportunities are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows the current version. Material changes will be posted on this page.

12. Contact
